Welcome to the Case Workflow
This app manages the full lifecycle of dental lab RX prescriptions — from the moment a PDF scan arrives to the point it's verified against the ABS production system and assigned a PAN number. Every case flows through a structured pipeline with built-in quality checks, team routing, and automated validation.

What This App Does

Dental offices send prescriptions (RX PDFs) via scanning portals. This app ingests those PDFs, extracts patient and case data, assigns them to team members for data entry into ABS, then automatically validates the entered data against ABS records. Cases that pass QC move to Verified status. Cases with discrepancies get flagged for review.

The Big Picture

The workflow has three phases:

1. PDF Ingestion Engine — PDFs are scanned, data is extracted, and cases enter the Available queue.

2. Data Entry & LMS — Team members pick up cases, enter them into the Lab Management System (LMS), and submit for QC.

3. Validation — The QC engine compares RX data against LMS records and either verifies the case or flags discrepancies for review.

Teams & Roles
The app uses a team-based structure. Each person belongs to a team and has a role that determines what they can see and do. Cases are assigned to teams, and team members pull work from their team's queue.

Role Levels

User — Can view their team's queue, pick up cases, and complete/route them. Sees only their assigned work in the focused user view.

Lead — Everything a User can do, plus: can see all team members' work, run the QC engine, perform QC reviews, and view workload across the team.

Admin — Full access to everything: all teams, all views, engine settings, team/role switching, case resets, and system configuration.

Level 1

Support Ninja (SN)

The front line. Level 1 users pull cases from the Available queue and enter them into ABS.

  • Pick up cases via "Assign Next Case"
  • Enter case data into ABS
  • Complete with case number, or route to Level 2
  • Can escalate to Level 2 OC
Level 2

Level 2 C&B OC

Handles escalated cases and routes to specialty teams. The command center for complex cases.

  • Reviews escalated cases from Level 1
  • Routes to AOX, Removables, or Onboarding
  • Can complete cases directly
  • Sees and manages Needs Attention column
AOX

AOX

Specialty team for AOX-specific restorations. Cases routed here by Level 2.

  • Sees only their team's queue
  • Complete or return to queue
  • Place on hold if needed
Removables

Removables

Specialty team for removable prosthetics. Same workflow pattern as AOX.

  • Sees only their team's queue
  • Complete or return to queue
  • Place on hold if needed
Onboarding

Onboarding

Handles cases from new or unrecognized accounts. When the engine can't match a doctor or office to an existing account, it auto-routes here. Completing an onboarding case requires entering the new account number.

How Teams See the App

Users see a focused view tailored to their team: a kanban column of their team's pending cases on the left, and a detail panel for the active case on the right. Leads and Admins see the full Kanban board with all columns.

3
PDF Ingestion
Cases enter the system when RX prescription PDFs are scanned from dental offices. The app can ingest PDFs automatically or on demand.
1

PDFs Arrive in SharePoint

Dental offices send prescriptions via scanning portals (3Shape, etc.). The PDF files land in a configured SharePoint/OneDrive folder.

2

Process Scans

An admin clicks "Process Scans" on the Kanban board (or it runs on a schedule). This triggers the ingestion engine which reads each PDF and extracts: patient name, doctor name, office name, material, shade, teeth, tooth count, and other RX details.

3

Engine Triage

The triage engine runs automatically on new cases. It looks up the doctor and office names against the Accounts table in Supabase. Based on the match quality, it sets flags and suggestions to help the data entry person.

4

Auto-Routing

If the engine can't find a matching account (new doctor/office), it can auto-route the case to the Onboarding team. Otherwise, the case lands in the Available queue for Level 1.

What Gets Extracted from the PDF

Patient first & last name, doctor name, office/practice name, material type, shade, tooth numbers, tooth count, reference number, delivery date, and source portal. This extracted data is what gets compared against ABS during QC validation later.

4
Level 1 Workflow
Level 1 (Support Ninja) users are the front line of data entry. They pick cases from the Available queue, open the RX PDF, enter the data into ABS, then complete the case with the ABS case number.
1

Assign Work (FIFO)

Click "Assign Work" to pull the next case. The system always assigns the oldest pending case in the queue (FIFO order). You cannot pick specific cases — this ensures fair distribution and prevents cherry-picking. SN staff are limited to one open case at a time per BU tab.

2

Review the RX

The case detail panel shows all extracted data: patient name, doctor, office, material, shade, teeth, and any Aspen triage flags. Click "Open PDF" to view the original prescription on the network.

3

Enter into ABS

Using the RX data, create the case in ABS (the production system). Enter all required fields: patient, doctor, account, material, teeth, etc.

4

Complete the Case

Click "Complete" and enter the ABS case number you just created. This triggers the QC engine to validate your entry against Supabase. The case moves to Pending QC.

Other Level 1 Actions

Escalate to OC Level II — If the case is complex or needs a more experienced team member, escalate it. The case moves to the Level 2 queue.

Hold — If you need to pause (waiting for info, etc.), place the case on hold. It moves to the Hold column and you can resume later.

Return to Queue — (If enabled by admin) Send the case back to Available so another team member can pick it up.

5
Level 2 C&B OC
Level 2 handles escalated cases and serves as the routing hub for specialty teams. L2 users have both workflow buttons (Return to Queue, Hold, Complete) and routing buttons (AOX, Removables, Onboard).

The L2 Button Bar

L2 users see two groups of buttons on their active case:

Workflow (left side): Return to Queue, Hold, Complete — standard case actions.
Route (right side): AOX, Removables, Onboard — sends the case to a specialty team.

When to Route

Route to AOX — The RX is for an AOX-specific restoration type.
Route to Removables — The RX is for removable prosthetics (dentures, partials, etc.).
Route to Onboarding — The doctor or office is new and needs to be set up as an account in ABS first.

Needs Attention Column

Level 2 also manages the Needs Attention column. Cases land here when the QC engine finds discrepancies between the RX data and ABS records. L2 users review the issues, fix or confirm them, and re-submit for QC validation.

6
Specialty Teams
AOX, Removables, and Onboarding are specialty teams that handle specific case types. They share a common view pattern: a kanban queue on the left with a detail panel on the right.

Specialty User View

Each specialty user sees only their team's cases. The layout mirrors the Level 1 view: click "Assign Next Case" to pull from the queue, review the case details, and take action.

Specialty teams have workflow buttons only (Return to Queue, Hold, Complete) — no routing buttons. If a case needs to go elsewhere, the lead or admin handles that.

AOX

AOX Workflow

AOX cases follow the same complete-with-case-number flow. Once completed, the case goes to Pending QC and the engine validates it against ABS just like any other case.

Removables

Removables Workflow

Identical pattern to AOX. Complete with a case number, and the QC engine takes over from there.

Onboarding

Onboarding Workflow

Onboarding is special: when completing a case, you must enter the new account number you created in ABS for this doctor/office. This is how the system learns about new accounts. The engine may auto-route cases here when it can't find a matching account during triage.

The QC Engine
The QC engine is the automated quality gate. After a case is completed (submitted with a case number), the engine looks up that case number in ABS and compares every RX field against what's in the production system.

What Gets Checked

FieldRX SourceABS Source
Patient First NameExtracted from PDFCases table
Patient Last NameExtracted from PDFCases table
Doctor NameExtracted from PDFAccounts table (First + Last Name)
Office / PracticeExtracted from PDFAccounts table (Practice Name)
Material / ProductExtracted from PDFCase Teeth table (Product)
Tooth NumbersExtracted from PDFCases table + Case Teeth rows
Tooth CountExtracted from PDFCases table

How Matching Works

The QC engine runs automatically via scheduled tasks — there's no manual "Run Engine" button. It uses intelligent matching behind the scenes: fuzzy name matching, material family detection, and tooth set comparison. When you confirm a discrepancy as "Non-Error," the engine learns that pairing globally and won't flag it again on future cases.

The 3-Strike Rule

If the engine can't find the case number in ABS, it doesn't immediately fail. It retries up to 3 times (cases may not be in ABS yet). After 3 failed lookups, the case is flagged as QC Failed and moves to Needs Attention.

8
Needs Attention & Verified
After QC runs, a case either passes and moves to Verified, or it has discrepancies and moves to Needs Attention. Here's how each outcome works.
!

Needs Attention

The QC engine found mismatches between RX and ABS data. The case shows a comparison table (RX vs ABS side by side) and lists each discrepancy. Each discrepancy has three options:

Confirmed Non-Error — The values are actually correct (e.g., different naming conventions). The engine learns this match for future cases.
Fixed — You corrected the ABS entry. The case will be re-checked.
Error — Still needs fixing. Can't re-submit until all errors are resolved.

After flagging all discrepancies, click "Re-submit to QC" to re-run validation.

P

Verified / Needs PAN

The case passed QC but doesn't have a PAN number yet. The app automatically checks ABS for PANs every 30 seconds when anyone has the board open. Once a PAN appears in ABS, it's assigned automatically — no manual action needed.

✓

Verified / PAN Assigned

The case is fully verified AND has a PAN number. This is the end state. The case stays visible based on the retention setting in Admin: either a fixed number of days, or "Same Work Day" mode where cases reset at 4am so only today's completions show on the board.

Copy QC Report

On any case with QC results, click "Copy Report" to copy a formatted summary to your clipboard. You can paste this directly into Microsoft Teams to share discrepancy details with your team.

Views & Filters
The app offers multiple ways to view cases, depending on whether you're checking overall workflow, individual workloads, or your own cases.

Workflow View (Kanban Board)

The default view. Shows all columns as a Kanban board. Each column represents a stage in the pipeline. Click any case card to open its full detail in a modal.

My Cases toggle — Filter the board to show only cases assigned to you. Available on Level 1, Level 2, and Hold columns.

By Person View (Heatmap)

A grid showing every team member as a row, with status columns across the top: Active, Hold, Pending QC, Attn, Verified, Needs PAN, plus Completed Today and Completed Yesterday. Staff are grouped by role (Support Ninja, OC, Onboarding). Click any cell to drill down to the case list.

By Status View

A grid showing case counts by BU (C&B, AOX, Removables, Onboarding) across all workflow statuses. Includes pipeline columns — Pending QC, Waiting for PAN, PAN Verified — with Today and Yesterday sub-counts for tracking daily throughput. Click any cell to see the case list.

User-Focused Views

When a regular user (not admin/lead) logs in, they see a focused view tailored to their team:

Level 1 users see: their active case detail, the Available queue summary, and their holds dropdown. Assignment is FIFO-only (oldest case first).

Level 2 users see: a kanban of L2 pending cases on the left, active case detail on the right, with workflow + routing buttons.

Specialty users (AOX, Removables, Onboarding) see: their team's queue on the left, active case detail on the right, with workflow-only buttons.

10
Admin Tools
The Admin tab provides system-wide configuration and tools. Only users with the Admin role can access these settings.

Engine & Workflow Settings

Auto-Escalate to L2 — When ON, cases that can't be matched to an account automatically escalate to Level 2.

Auto-Route to Onboarding — When ON, cases with no matching account get automatically routed to the Onboarding team.

SN Return to Queue — When ON, Level 1 users can send their assigned case back to the Available queue.

OC Max Open Cases — Maximum number of cases an OC/Level 2 member can have assigned at once (default: 5). Support Ninja is always limited to 1.

Verified Retention — Choose "Days" to keep verified cases visible for a set number of days, or "Same Work Day" to reset at 4am so only today's completions show on the board. The By Status grid still tracks yesterday's PAN Verified count regardless.

Folder Settings

Configure the SharePoint/network paths where PDFs are stored, where completed PDFs go, and the post-QC checked folder. These paths are used by the Process Scans function.

Deep QC

A batch validation tool that checks ALL cases with case numbers against the full ABS data set — Cases table, Case Teeth table, and Accounts table. Runs patient name, tooth numbers, tooth count, material, doctor, and office checks across every case at once.

View As (Team/Role Switcher)

Admins can switch their view to see the app as any team/role combination would see it. Useful for testing views, training new team members, or troubleshooting what a specific user sees.
